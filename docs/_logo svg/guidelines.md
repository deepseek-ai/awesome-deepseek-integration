# DeepSeek Brand Asset Usage Guidelines
These guidelines provide developers with guidance for descriptive and nominative use of DeepSeek brand assets. They do NOT constitute a license or authorization of DeepSeek’s trademark rights, copyrights or other intellectual property rights. Developers shall use brand assets reasonably in compliance with these guidelines. We reserve the right to take legal action against any use that falls outside the scope of these guidelines, fails to comply with the principles of fair use, or may infringe DeepSeek’s legitimate rights and interests.
Please note the following:
- All use of brand assets must be legitimate and necessary, limited solely to descriptive and nominative use. Trademark use is prohibited.
- Use DeepSeek assets accurately and appropriately. Do not use incorrect, counterfeit or low-quality assets, and do not alter them without authorization.
- You may use these brand assets only if you have actually integrated DeepSeek models or APIs, or adopted unmodified DeepSeek open-source front-end code. Do not engage in false advertising or intentionally induce the false impression that you have a partnership with DeepSeek. The use of “DeepSeek” or model names in application names or website domain names is prohibited, as this may mislead end users.
- Do not use DeepSeek brand assets to impersonate the official DeepSeek entity, or create ambiguity or implication that may mislead others into believing you are affiliated with the official DeepSeek team.
- You must comply with applicable laws governing intellectual property rights including trademarks and copyrights when using brand assets.
